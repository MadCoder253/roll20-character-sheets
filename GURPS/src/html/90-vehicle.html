Vehicle Statistics

Type Enter a description for the type of vehicle.
  • Ground
  • Ground/Road
  • Ground/Rail
  • Watercraft
  • Aircraft
  • Spacecraft
  • Amphibious
  • Etc.
TL Enter the Tech Level of the vehicle.
ST Enter the Strength of the Vehicle. An unpowered vehicle will have a Strength of zero. B462.
HP Enter the Hit Points of the Vehicle. B462.
SR Vehicle Stability Rating determines if the failed Control Roll is a minor or major failure, B466.
HT Vehicle's Health. B466. Use the check-boxes to the right to identify fragile features of the vehicle. Unpowered vehicles are Homogenous with HT 12, while machines have HT 10, Damage to Objects, B483.
Modifiers:
  • Cheap, temperamental, or poorly maintained, -1 to -3 HT.
  • Well-made or rugged, +1 or +2 HT.
Accel Enter the vehicle's acceleration in yards (hexes) per second. B463.
Top Enter the vehicle's top speed in yards per second. B463.
Lwt Enter the vehicle's loaded weight, in tons, of the vehicle. This is the maximum payload and a full load of fuel. B463.
Cap Vehicle's cargo capacity, uses the formula of Load subtract weight of occupants, where each person is .1 tons. B463.
Curb Vehicle's curb weight (with fuel but no other payload), which is Loaded Weight minus Load. B463.
SM Vehicle's size modifier, B463.
Crew Enter the number of crew members needed to operate the vehicle. B463.
Passengers Enter the number of crew members needed to operate the vehicle. B463.
DR Enter the vehicle's Damage Resistance, this can be used as a reference when building hit locations. B463.
Range The travel distance before the vehicle runs of out fuel or provisions. B463.
Cruise % Enter a percentage to calculate the vehicle's Cruising speed, which is typically about 60 to 70 percent of the top speed. B463.
End. Vehicle's endurance in hours is the Range divided by Cruising speed. For situations where "Loiter" capability matters more than range. Vehicle must carry provisions in order to take advantage of endurance in excess of one day. B463.
Cost Vehicle's cost. B463.
Loc Vehicle's hit locations, this can be used to help build the hit locations table. B463. The quantity precedes the abbreviation; e.g., "3M" for a three-masted ship.
Hit Location Abbreviations. B463.
  • A: arm
  • C: caterpillar tracks
  • D: draft animals
  • E: exposed rider
  • G: large glass windows
  • g: small glass windows
  • H: helicopter rotors
  • L: leg
  • M: mast and rigging
  • O: open cabin
  • R: runners or skids
  • r: retractable
  • S: large superstructure or gondola
  • s: small superstructure
  • T: main turret
  • t: independent turret
  • W: wheel
  • Wi: a pair of wings
  • X: exposed weapon mount
Draft For a watercraft, the minimum depth of water it can safely operate in. B463.
Stall For an aircraft, the minimum speed, in hex (yards)/second, it must maint to take off and stay airborne. "0" means it can hover. B463.
G For spacecraft, the accel in Earth gravities (G), calculated as acceleration divided by 10. B463.

Enter the amount of damage this location has taken.
NOTE: If you want this column to affect the current Vehicle Hit Points,
click the checkbox for Sync HP.

Div

Hit Point Divisor. If the number is 2 or higher, that means the location can be disabled and a value will be updated in the next column. See B421.

Thresh

Disabled/Crippled Threshold.
If a single attack meets or exceeds this value, the extremity or structure is disabled.
NOTE: In most cases, if disabled/crippled in one injury, the excess damage is lost.
Dismemberment: If injury is twice the threshold, then extremity is destroyed. Cutting or Explosion will sever the limb.
See B421.

Dis

Certain location must make a HT if they suffer damage. If failed, check this box. If vehicle location exceedes the Hit Point Threshold, mark the location as disabled.

Leak

Check this box if the location is leaking.

Fire

Check this box if the location is on fire.

Speed %

Enter the percentage of change to the top speed when the location is disabled. This will automatically update the top speed. For example, if you enter 25, the top speed will be reduced by 25%. The total percentage can't exceed 100%.

DR

Enter the Damage Resistance for this hit location. Use the Notes text box to keep track of the original DR if armor is damaged.

N-Scale D-Scale C-Scale

The scaled value of the hit location's damage resistance. Will automatically update when the scale is selected.

Hits

Enter individual hits here, if limb is crippled, burning, etc.
Example: -6 pi (major wound), -2 burn.
