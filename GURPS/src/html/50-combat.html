Verge of Collapse
- Roll every turn @{VS} HT or fall unconscious.
- Your HT roll is at -1 for every 1xHP you are below 0.
- If you do nothing, and don't defend, you don't have to roll.
Verge of Death
- Roll once @{VS} HT to stay alive.
- If you fail by 1 or 2 you are mortally wounded, but not dead. (B423)
- Roll again every time you take injury that puts you below
another multiple of your max HP.

Large-Area Injury
Large-Area Injury Attacks that affect most of the body; dragon's breath, bomb blast, huge fire, acid pit, or attacher that has Size Modifier Difference of 7 or higher compared to defennder. If DR varies by hit location, determine effective DR using average of torsor DR and the DR of least protected hit location exposed to the attack. If DR varies by damage type, use the least protected value. A location protected by cover or masked by the body does not count as exposed to the attack. For example, if you're back is turned to a cone attack by a dragon, your face is not exposed to the attack. Ignore special hit-location efects. B400.

RoF
Rate of Fire
The maximum number of shots that can be fired in
one turn (by a regular person).
The minimum number of shots is 1.

!: minimum RoF is 1/4 of the maximum, rounded up.
mxn: fires the first number of shots, which each then
split into the second number (eg. shotguns)
Jet: shoots a continuous stream of fluid. See page 106.
Shots
The number of shots that can be fired before the
weapon must be reloaded.
The parenthetical number indicates the number of 1
second ready maneuvers required to reload the
weapon.
T: the weapon is thrown. To "reload" it, pick it up.
i: you must load the shots individually. the listed
time is for each shot.
RCL
When firing at RoF 2+, every full multiple of Rcl
by which you make your attack roll means you score
one extra hit, to a maximum number of hits equal
to total shots fired. See page 373.

Slam requires an Attack, All-out Attack, or Move and Attack maneuver. Your foe may block, dodge, or parry (but your body counts as a heavy weapon; see Parrying Heavy Weapons B376). Weight
Calculated weight of the explosive charge.
Brick Wall Formula: (0.5 x (diameter of the desiered hole in inches) x (thickness of the wall in inches)) Squared = required weight of TNT (REF 1) in lbs.
Concrete Wall Formula: (1.0 x (diameter of the desiered hole in inches) x (thickness of the wall in inches)) Squared = required weight of TNT (REF 1) in lbs.
Iron Plate Formula: (2 x (diameter of the desiered hole in inches) x (thickness of the wall in inches)) Squared = required weight of TNT (REF 1) in lbs.
Steel Plate Formula: (2.5 x (diameter of the desiered hole in inches) x (thickness of the wall in inches)) Squared = required weight of TNT (REF 1) in lbs.
